Questions restaurants ask us

Do you take a commission on orders?

No. You pay one flat monthly fee. The only per-order cost is the card processing fee from Stripe, which goes to Stripe, not us.

Can I keep Uber Eats and DoorDash?

Yes. Many restaurants keep marketplaces for new customers and move regulars to their own app and website, where they keep the full margin.

What kitchen printer do I need?

Standard thermal kitchen printers (such as Epson or Star) work. If you don't have one, we help you pick and set it up.

Do I have to change my POS?

No. You can run on our dashboard and kitchen printer alone. Lightspeed and Square sync is included in the Pro plan.

How long does it take to go live?

Usually one to three weeks, depending on menu size. App store review can add a few days for the app.

Is there a contract?

No lock-in. Pay monthly and cancel any time. Your customer data stays yours.
