Our story

From running operations to automating them

Our founder spent 8+ years at Bikroy.com — Bangladesh's largest online marketplace — leading large operations teams with full P&L responsibility.

That job showed how the wrong digital systems slow businesses down, and how the right ones unlock growth: fewer phone calls, fewer spreadsheets, more time for customers.

MS Biz Studio was built in Sweden from that understanding. Today we work with restaurants, pharmacies, retailers and service businesses in four countries.
