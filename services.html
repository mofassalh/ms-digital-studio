Common questions

How long does a project take?

Most websites launch in 2–4 weeks. Automation, CRM and app projects take 4–10 weeks depending on scope. You get a timeline with your quote.

Every project is different, so after a free call we send a fixed written quote — no surprise hourly bills. Restaurant ordering systems are a flat monthly fee.

Yes. Your code, domain and data belong to you.

What happens after launch?

We train your team and offer ongoing support, reporting and improvements.
